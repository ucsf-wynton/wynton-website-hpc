### September 7-8, 2026

#### <span class="incident-title">Full downtime</span>

**Notice**: Wynton HPC will undergo a full scheduled downtime,
including login, development, data-transfer, and application nodes,
beginning Monday afternoon, September 7 (Labor Day; exact time TBD),
through 5:00 PM on Tuesday, September 8, 2026. UCSF Facilities will
perform required annual fire-inspection activities, and the Wynton
team will take the opportunity to implement kernel updates. Jobs with
runtime limits (h_rt) that extend into the maintenance window will not
be started until after the downtime has concluded.  <br><span
class="timestamp">August 12, 01:00 PT</span>
